Understanding EMI

❓ What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed amount paid every month until the loan is fully repaid.

🧮 EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = P × R × (1+R)^N / ((1+R)^N -1)

P = Principal Amount
R = Monthly Interest Rate
N = Number of Months

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI is a fixed monthly payment made towards loan repayment.

How EMI is calculated?

EMI depends on loan amount, interest rate and tenure.

Can I reduce my EMI?

Yes, by increasing tenure or making higher down payment.

How interest rate affects EMI?

Higher interest rate increases your monthly EMI.
